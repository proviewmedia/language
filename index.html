How it works

A smarter way to learn

Three steps. No account required. Start speaking in minutes.

See it

Each phrase appears with a clear English translation. You see the Spanish, understand the meaning, and learn the pattern — no guessing games.

Hear it

Every phrase has clear, native-accent audio at natural and slowed speed. Tap to hear it, then hear it again. Your ear trains alongside your brain.

Use it

You get an English prompt and say the Spanish out loud yourself, then hear the correct pronunciation and rate how you did. No multiple choice, no typing — you produce it. That's what makes it stick.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

First Words, Familiar Words, the 5 Magic Frames, and Eating & Drinking are free. Here's the full course.

Arrival & the Airport

Passport, baggage, the exit, a SIM or taxi. Get through the airport and out the door on your own.

Getting Around

Tell the driver where you're going, ask the fare, say "stop here." Get where you're going and pay a fair price.

Checking In

Reservation, breakfast, reporting a problem. Check in, ask about breakfast, and fix a room issue.

Eating & Drinking — Free

Get a table, order, handle allergies, ask what's good, and pay. Eat like a local, not a tourist pointing at pictures.

Shopping & Money

Ask the price, sizes, the polite haggle, card or cash. Ask prices, try things on, and pay your way.

Directions

Ask where, left/right/straight, near/far/blocks. Ask for directions and actually follow the answer.

Trouble

Pharmacy, feeling sick, lost or stolen, a doctor. Get help when you're sick, hurt, or something's lost.

Connecting

Greet warmly, small talk, be a good guest. Break the ice and be a gracious guest.
