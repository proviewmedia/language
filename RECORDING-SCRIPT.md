# EspTalk — Recording Script (v2 · new course)

Record every line **twice**: once at **natural** speed, once **slow** (deliberate, with clear gaps to repeat). Name files by the number below; add `-slow` for the slow take (e.g. `007.m4a` and `007-slow.m4a`). Latin American neutral, quiet room, consistent mic.

Same phrase used in two modules is listed once, at first appearance.
