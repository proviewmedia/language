EspTalk — The Full Curriculum

Travel Spanish that actually works · what a learner goes through, start to finish
The promise: get a motivated beginner to survival-conversational (A1+) before they land — able to handle arrival, transit, lodging, food, shopping, directions, and trouble, and to recombine frames to say things they were never explicitly taught. Not fluent. Functional. Taught by a real human voice.
How every module works — the same rhythm, so it always makes sense:
🎬 Scene (hear it live)→ 👂 Present→ 🔁 Listen & Repeat→ 🧩 Frame swap→ 🗣️ Produce (say it)→ 🎭 Role-play→ ✅ Spoken checkpoint
Receptive → guided → productive → applied. No typing, no guessing. Each module opens with a 2-minute spoken warm-up of earlier material.

✓Engine Checkpoint
A spoken review, not a quiz. Eight real situations by audio ("you want a coffee," "ask where the train is," "you need help") — say a correct sentence for each. Pass at 6 of 8. This proves recombination, the only thing that matters.
